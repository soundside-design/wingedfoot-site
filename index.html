Wingedfoot

A private voice line to the AI agent running on hardware your household owns.

Wingedfoot is an iPhone app that talks to an appliance your family runs — a computer in your home, not a service we operate. Conversations go to that machine. Soundside Design runs no backend that receives them.

How it works

  • The line is full-duplex and interruptible. Speak whenever you want, and interrupting actually stops the agent, not just the audio.
  • Every call has a live written transcript. Nothing said aloud is only aloud.
  • Each person in the household gets their own agent, their own memory, and their own credentials — not a shared login.
  • A phone joins by scanning a short-lived code and being approved by the household admin. No VPN app required.

Requires an appliance

This is a companion app. Without a household appliance running the Wingedfoot server, it has nothing to connect to.

Status

Wingedfoot is in private beta with a small number of households. There is no public sign-up yet.
